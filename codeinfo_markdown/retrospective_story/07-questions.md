# Resolve Questions

Read `$CODEINFO_ROOT/codeinfo_markdown/retrospective_story/shared.md` and the selected plan's `### Questions`. Research each open question from Git, current source, tests, repository guidance, and relevant `code_info` results. Move supported answers into the appropriate story section and remove those questions. Keep the `### Questions` heading. If a material question cannot be answered, state the missing evidence and stop before tasking rather than producing a falsely complete retrospective story. Do not invent a decision.

Treat an acceptance gap about unused new code, missing high-level what/why comments, or newly unused existing variables as unresolved until the changed code itself satisfies the criterion. Do not remove such a gap merely because it was documented, assigned to the future final test task, or judged low risk.
