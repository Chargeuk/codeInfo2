# Repeated-finding repair handoff

The current batch may contain a history-matching and research record under `reconciliation/` (normally `repeated-findings.md`). Discover and interpret its meaning, exact batch identity, and provenance using `shared/review-artifact-handoff.md`; do not require a rigid filename or schema. Older batches predating this stage do not require retroactive placeholder artifacts. In a current execution that schedules this stage, missing expected evidence requires recovery, not a clean skip. Trustworthy no-accepted-findings evidence makes the whole repair stage inapplicable; a complete no-candidates result makes only repeated research inapplicable.

Read this alongside current disposition and Git state. Historical `Accepted` means previously seen, not necessarily fixed; history is not authority. Current accepted actionable findings remain the scope. Never restore earlier gate removals.

- Ordinary repair owns findings positively routed as new and candidates research disproved as historical matches.
- Repeated research owns possible repeats and unresolved matching uncertainty. Normal and stronger fixers must not redo its confirmed/uncertain candidates, including unresolved ones, in the same batch. Carry their research evidence forward explicitly rather than reporting them as normal-agent fixes or forgetting them.
- A trustworthy research fix or already-resolved outcome contributes to combined completion. Completion requires evidence that every accepted actionable finding is resolved, including research-owned findings; unresolved/uncertain research outcomes never justify a clean completion break.
- Missing or partial routing/research evidence must be recovered from trustworthy current-batch evidence and history where possible. Continue clearly ordinary work; preserve unclassified or research-owned remainders as unavailable/partial research coverage for settlement, rather than silently routing them through the old failing implementation path. An unavailable research attempt is not an additional normal/stronger repair opportunity for those findings.
- The research invocation is the stronger repair opportunity for its assigned findings. After it has run or was honestly unavailable, its unresolved actionable remainder is eligible for existing complete-pass settlement without a second stronger attempt. Gate conflicts remain conflicts, not implementation work. Settlement task descriptions must carry prior repairs, reversal reasons, approaches exhausted, and the reconciled requirements so later implementation does not restart blindly.
- Count repairs by any of the repeated-research, normal, or stronger stages in batch outcomes, completed-review-fix tasks, continuation decisions, and final testing coverage. A research-only repair commit makes the batch fix-bearing. Record exact per-repository commits and proof and review newly committed HEADs as usual. Unresolved repeats explicitly left for settlement do not by themselves keep the repeated group running.

This is one bounded research opportunity per batch, not a permanent issue ledger, new grouping algorithm, human approval step, or guarantee that the outer story loop will always terminate. Preserve the self-describing record as historical evidence for future recurrence investigation.
