## Ambiguous-delivery launch reconciliation

Apply the **ambiguous-delivery** exception only to a launch for which delivery/acceptance and provider acknowledgement cannot be established for that exact candidate/reviewer. For that reviewer only, observation may become unavailable after **three completed successful reconciliation/readback observations**, each separated by the existing native **300-second** polling wait, with no confirmed delivery/acceptance or provider acknowledgement. Each eligible successful reconciliation must inspect actual live evidence attributable to the exact candidate/reviewer across supported **request/trigger/comment/check/review** sources. Failed API queries and empty `reviewRequests` alone do not count as completed observations or establish non-delivery or completion. Record each completed readback and its evidence in the existing assigned work evidence; add no file, schema, timer, or state mechanism. Never retry the review request.

Once delivery/acceptance or provider acknowledgement or pending/running is confirmed for this candidate/reviewer, **this exception never applies**, even if status later becomes unknown. Such pending, running, and unknown states keep waiting without a general timeout. While any sibling remains pending, running or unknown without its own applicable ambiguous-delivery exception, **answer no** and continue the loop. A terminal outcome or qualifying unavailable observation for one reviewer cannot end another sibling's wait. Unavailable observation is **not a terminal review or a clean result**; preserve it as a per-reviewer coverage limitation alongside useful sibling findings and continue ordinary best-effort batch processing.

The flow performs an immediate observation, then uses the flow's native 300-second wait before polling again. Continue waiting for all requested reviewers to reach a terminal outcome, whether successful or unsuccessful, except that an individual ambiguous-delivery launch may become unavailable under the three-readback rule below. Do not shell-sleep or busy-loop. Pending, running, and unknown remain nonterminal and keep observation waiting until their state changes or a factual inability to observe that reviewer is established, subject only to that same unconfirmed-launch exception. Once delivery/acceptance, provider acknowledgement, or pending/running is confirmed, the exception never applies, even if status later becomes unknown. When observation of one reviewer becomes genuinely unavailable, preserve that limitation and continue waiting for siblings that remain observable; do not fabricate a terminal reviewer outcome or current clean coverage.

Reopen and update the existing assigned evidence with the fresh status snapshot and per-reviewer state. State exact candidate/head, all statuses, evidence paths, and limits; do not claim clean coverage on unknown or stale evidence.
