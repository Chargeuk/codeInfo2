# Repeated-finding repair handoff

The current batch may contain a history-matching and research record under `reconciliation/` (normally `repeated-findings.md`). Discover and interpret its meaning, exact batch identity, and provenance using `shared/review-artifact-handoff.md`; do not require a rigid filename or schema. Older batches predating this stage do not require retroactive placeholder artifacts. In a current execution that schedules this stage, missing expected evidence requires bounded recovery, not a clean coverage claim. Trustworthy current-batch evidence of an empty accepted actionable set makes the whole repair stage inapplicable even when unrelated history or review coverage is incomplete. A complete no-eligible-repeat result makes only Astra research inapplicable.

Read this alongside current disposition and Git state. Historical `Accepted` means previously seen, not necessarily fixed; history is not authority. Current accepted actionable findings remain the scope. Never restore earlier gate removals.

- Ordinary repair owns findings positively routed as new, historical matches without finding-specific attempted-repair evidence, and candidates research disproved as historical matches.
- Astra research owns only current accepted actionable findings that plausibly match a historical Accepted record and have trustworthy evidence of an attempted repair for that same underlying behavior. Rejected, ignored, legacy-unclassified, or merely same-task/same-ID history never qualifies. Matching or coverage uncertainty alone does not invoke Astra: route independent current work ordinarily, or retain a genuinely unavailable remainder for settlement. Normal and stronger fixers must not redo an Astra-owned candidate, including an unresolved one, in the same batch. Carry research evidence forward explicitly rather than reporting it as normal-agent work or forgetting it.
- A trustworthy research fix or already-resolved outcome contributes to combined completion. Completion requires evidence that every accepted actionable finding is resolved, including research-owned findings; unresolved/uncertain research outcomes never justify a clean completion break.
- Missing or partial routing/research evidence must be recovered from trustworthy current-batch evidence and history where possible. Continue clearly ordinary work; preserve a genuinely unavailable remainder as an explicit settlement limitation rather than silently escalating uncertainty to Astra or claiming clean coverage. An unavailable eligible-research attempt is not an additional normal/stronger repair opportunity for its findings.
- The research invocation is the stronger repair opportunity for its assigned findings. After it has run or was honestly unavailable, its unresolved actionable remainder is eligible for existing complete-pass settlement without a second stronger attempt. Gate conflicts remain conflicts, not implementation work. Settlement task descriptions must carry prior repairs, reversal reasons, approaches exhausted, and the reconciled requirements so later implementation does not restart blindly.
- Count repairs by any of the repeated-research, normal, or stronger stages in batch outcomes, completed-review-fix tasks, continuation decisions, and final testing coverage. A research-only repair commit makes the batch fix-bearing. Record exact per-repository commits and proof and review newly committed HEADs as usual. Unresolved repeats explicitly left for settlement do not by themselves keep the repeated group running.

This is one bounded research opportunity per batch, not a permanent issue ledger, new grouping algorithm, human approval step, or guarantee that the outer story loop will always terminate. Preserve the self-describing record as historical evidence for future recurrence investigation.
