## Ambiguous-delivery launch reconciliation

Apply the **ambiguous-delivery** exception only to a launch for which delivery/acceptance and provider acknowledgement cannot be established for that exact candidate/reviewer. Make one available, supported reconciliation/readback attempt across documented sources; a failed or denied source is unavailable and must not be retried. If no trustworthy evidence resolves the launch, record it unavailable with the evidence limitation and do not retry or duplicate the request. Empty `reviewRequests` alone does not establish non-delivery or completion. Record the readback in existing assigned work evidence; add no file, schema, timer, or state mechanism.

Confirmation of delivery/acceptance, provider acknowledgement, or pending/running establishes that a launch was made, but does not require indefinite waiting if later observation becomes unknown or its source/access route is unavailable. A single failure of a repository-provided observation mechanism makes that route unavailable for this operation; do not retry it on re-entry or use it to hold the batch. Continue native polling only for reviewers with observable active pending/running work or a supported, useful next observation. Preserve unknown or inaccessible reviewer coverage as unavailable rather than clean, and continue with observable siblings. Never duplicate or relaunch a confirmed request in this step or a later pass for the unchanged candidate.

Do not shell-sleep or busy-loop. Unknown or missing evidence alone is not proof of active work and must not cause indefinite waits. State the evidence that makes the next wait useful. When observation of one reviewer becomes unavailable, preserve that limitation and continue observing siblings that remain available; do not fabricate a terminal outcome or current clean coverage.

Reopen and update the existing assigned evidence with the fresh status snapshot and per-reviewer state. State exact candidate/head, all statuses, evidence paths, and limits; do not claim clean coverage on unknown or stale evidence.
